# Sustainable Finance: Green Technology Meets Money

The intersection of environmental sustainability and financial technology is creating new opportunities for both investors and the planet.

## The Rise of Green Finance

Sustainable finance encompasses various approaches to environmentally conscious money management:

### ESG Investing
Environmental, Social, and Governance factors are becoming central to investment decisions:
- **Climate risk assessment** in portfolio management
- **Social impact measurement** of investments
- **Corporate governance** evaluation criteria
- **Long-term value creation** focus

### Green Bonds
Debt securities specifically earmarked for environmental projects:
- **Renewable energy** project financing
- **Energy efficiency** improvements
- **Sustainable transportation** infrastructure
- **Clean water** and waste management systems
